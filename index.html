How to install & use offline

Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open the app link (an https:// address) in Chrome once while you have internet or Wi-Fi.
  2. Wait until the top shows “Offline ready” (it downloads about 12 MB once: the OCR reader and FCCC lists).
  3. Tap ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open Kishore Sell from the home screen. It now works in airplane mode.
  5. Tap Scan → Take photo once while online to be sure the reader is cached (the OCR pill turns green).

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the https link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Open once online, then it works offline.

Laptop

  1. Unzip kishore-offline.zip. In that folder run python3 -m http.server 8765 and open http://localhost:8765 in Chrome/Edge → install icon in the address bar.
  2. Or just double-click index.html. Pricing, FCCC lookup, history and OCR work; install/offline-cache needs http(s).

Using it

  1. Scan: take a photo. The app reads it on the phone and fills the review table.
  2. Always check the table against the photo (tap the photo to zoom). Fix item, qty, FOC, cost, disc %, VAT G/B, category. Add or delete lines.
  3. FCCC column: green ✓ = matched with high confidence (applied). Orange ⚠ = check and tap to choose, or mark “Not FCCC”. Grey = not FCCC.
  4. Press Price all. Sell prices show in red. Copy or Share the sell list into Viber.
  5. Invoices are saved in History on this phone.

Locked pricing rules

  • Cost = net VEP after discount %. FOC of the same item blends into unit cost: total cost ÷ (qty + FOC).
  • VAT 12.5% added LAST, only on G items. B = zero-rated.
  • $5 freight per carton/bag, except Coca-Cola Europacific and FMF group (FMF Foods, FMF Snax, Biscuit Company, The Rice Company, Pea Industries). Never in FCCC cost.
  • Non-FCCC: standard 30% · spices 100% · Chow 5×80g / Maggi 80g 15% · Coke 25% no freight · jasmine rice 12% no freight · meat/lamb 20% per kg (lamb sausage 30%): cost/kg + $5 ÷ kg/ctn.
  • FCCC: OI markup % = OI max retail ÷ VL urban max wholesale (converted to the same retail pack) − 1. Retail RRP = unit cost × (1 + OI markup). Also shows FCCC OI RP (cap).
  • FMF RRP always added: FMF rice 7%, FMF flour 8%, coarse besan 10%, pea besan 10% (VAT last only if G).
  • Selling units: garlic per 400g · apples/oranges/pears per piece · eggs per tray (5 trays/ctn + $5) · Coke shows pack per carton.

New FCCC price list?

  1. Download the new list PDF from fccc.gov.fj on the phone.
  2. Go to FCCC → Update FCCC list (open it), choose the PDF.
  3. Check the date, counts and price changes in the preview, then tap Use this list. It works offline.
  4. To go back to an older list: FCCC → Saved lists (open) → Use this.
  5. If the PDF won't read (FCCC changed the layout), use Upload a CSV with the template.
  6. Biscuit (29 Oct 2025) and wheat flour (28 Feb 2025) authorisations are built in and are not uploaded — they only change when FCCC issues a new authorisation.
